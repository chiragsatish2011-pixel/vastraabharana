# Vastra Abharana — Haute Couture Shopify OS 2.0 Theme Package

This turnkey theme package equips your Shopify storefront (`vastraabharana.com` / `vastrabharana.myshopify.com`) with the **Materia Themes luxury streetwear/editorial aesthetic** tailored for Royal Mysore Silk and heritage Indian ethnic wear.

Built strictly on **Shopify Online Store 2.0 (OS 2.0)** JSON template architecture (`templates/index.json` + `sections/*.liquid`), giving the store owner 100% visual customization control inside the standard Shopify Theme Editor while preserving all native Shopify backend connections (AJAX cart, Shopify checkout, dynamic collections, multi-currency, and inventory).

---

1. **Hero Stage (`BE THE DESI GIRL`)**:
   - High-impact glowing typography: **`BE THE`** on line 1, **`DESI GIRL`** on line 2.

2. **Cursive Signature Header Island**:
   - "Vastra Abharana" brand logo in haute couture cursive script (*Alex Brush*), perfectly dead-centered in the page layout.

3. **Curated Artisan Collections Bento Grid**:
   - Asymmetrical 4-card editorial bento grid (Sarees, Accessories, Salwar Suits, Combos).
   - Fully customizable in Shopify Customizer: pick any store collection, change images, titles, and links.

4. **Featured Products Catalog**:
   - Responsive 2-to-5 column product grid with hover overlay and instant double-click navigation.
   - Integrated with Shopify native `collections` picker, price formatting, discount badges, and AJAX Add-to-Bag.

5. **Spring 2026 Ready to Wear Showcase**:
   - 3 contiguous hairline-divided rectangles matching high-fashion editorial lookbooks.
   - Live brand storytelling and collection callouts.

6. **Welded Archive 2026 & Festive Diwali Section**:
   - Cinematic scroll-triggered dark shutter rectangles that part smoothly as the visitor scrolls into view.

   - 3 authentic trust badges: 100% Genuine Silk Mark Certified, Direct Weaver Fair-Trade Commerce, Pure Gold Zari Heirloom Weaves.

7. **Full-Screen Luxury Shopping Bag & AJAX Drawer**:
   - Connected to native Shopify endpoints: `/cart.js`, `/cart/add.js`, `/cart/change.js`, and `/checkout`.
   - Free Express Delivery progress indicator and real-time subtotal/total calculations.

## ✦ Shopify Online Store 2.0 Modular Template System

The store owner can modify the website design at any time with **zero coding knowledge** using the visual Shopify Theme Customizer:

### How to Customize Sections in Shopify Admin:
1. Go to **Shopify Admin** (`https://vastrabharana.myshopify.com/admin`) > **Online Store** > **Themes**.
2. Click **Customize** on the active *Vastra Abharana Materia* theme.
3. In the left sidebar, click any section to edit its settings live:
   - **Hero Editorial 3D**: Change top line (`BE THE`), bottom line (`DESI GIRL`), button label (`SHOP NOW`), or destination link.
   - **Artisan Collections Bento**: Select your featured collections, upload custom editorial photography, change card titles.
   - **Featured Products Catalog**: Choose which Shopify collection to showcase, set the number of products to show (4 to 25), customize badge text.
   - **Spring Showcase Editorial**: Update the story text, headlines, and left/right lookbook photos.
   - **Brand Metrics Counter**: Update your follower count, happy patrons, or weaver statistics.
   - **Welded Archive 2026**: Customize the script title ("Happy Diwali"), year text ("2026"), badge labels, and button destination.
   - **Header Sticky Island**: Customize the cursive logo text and subtitle.
   - **Footer Editorial**: Update about copy, legal entity details, and copyright notices.
4. **Reorder Sections**: Drag and drop any section up or down in the sidebar to change the page layout instantly.
5. Click **Save** in the top right corner. All changes take effect immediately!

## ✦ How to Upload the Theme Zip (3 Easy Steps)

1. **Locate the Theme Zip**:
   - Use `vastraabharana-materia-theme.zip` located in your project root directory.
2. **Upload to Shopify Admin**:
   - Go to your **Shopify Admin** (`https://vastrabharana.myshopify.com/admin`).
   - Click **Online Store** > **Themes**.
   - In the "Theme library" section, click **Add theme** > **Upload zip file**.
   - Select `vastraabharana-materia-theme.zip` and click **Upload file**.
3. **Preview & Publish**:
   - Click **Actions** > **Preview** to verify the live look with your store's real products.
   - When ready, click **Publish** to make it your active live storefront!

*Note: Your existing products, collections, inventory, customer orders, payment gateways, and apps remain 100% untouched and fully operational.*
